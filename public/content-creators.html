Content Creation Is a Different Kind of Business

Content creators don't always have the same expenses, or the same clear lines between business and personal as a traditional business.

A hardware store buys inventory. A contractor buys tools. For a content creator, an ordinary purchase can become part of producing income: equipment, software, props, products used in content, travel, subscriptions, or even a portion of certain expenses that have both business and personal use.

That doesn't mean everything you feature in a video is automatically deductible.

Knowing the difference matters.

DATAccounting helps creators understand what expenses may qualify as business deductions, what documentation should be kept, and how expenses with both business and personal use should be tracked and allocated.

Proper bookkeeping isn't just about finding deductions. It's about being able to support those deductions if the IRS ever asks.

The goal is simple: identify the deductions you're entitled to, understand what it takes to support them, and keep the records necessary to claim them confidently.
